    Read before relying

    A careful packet is not a truth certificate.

    The builder assembles instructions. It does not run the Python checker, verify citations, authenticate evidence, execute theorem provers, or decide whether a scientific theory is true.

    Do not upload secrets, identifying records, controlled information, or material you are not allowed to share. Prefer a local approved workflow for sensitive targets.
