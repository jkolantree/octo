## Preserved R01 forensic result

The profile-`2a2a4248` Case 27 run remains a negative result. Its mathematics
passed, but execution and representation consistency failed. The visible
response, returned metadata, and exported payload did not carry one consistent
runtime/byte identity. That substantive contradiction is `candidate_failed`;
later controller or transport findings do not turn it into a pass.

The original Return Desk replay supplied five generated outputs but omitted the
exact case target and all six canonical Knowledge files. It is therefore
`trial_invalid_controller` and is neither a candidate pass nor a candidate
failure. A later complete-roster replay narrowed the remaining Return Desk
finding without erasing the independently observed candidate contradiction.

match the declared 11,527-byte payload. A clean retry decoded to 11,527 bytes
with SHA-256
`ce9bc53431459a903de78b75b3c08af3550150731c17c0ff2fed38ab4b6bd5cf`.
Downstream decoding was exact, including detection of an aligned omitted
quartet, `ZW5k` (`end`). Original download-button bytes were unavailable, so
their identity is `transport_identity_unresolved`; browser/download corruption
was not established.
