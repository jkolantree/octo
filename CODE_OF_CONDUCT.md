# Code of Conduct

The project welcomes rigorous disagreement, unusual ideas, compact counterexamples, and direct criticism of claims. Participation requires respect for people and precision about evidence.

## Expected conduct

- Critique claims, proofs, code, and procedures rather than a person’s identity or worth.
- State uncertainty and preserve disagreement.
- Credit sources and contributors accurately.
- Protect confidential and identifying information.
- Accept valid counterexamples and prospective demotions.
- Make review accessible to people with different backgrounds and communication styles.
- Avoid status, credentials, novelty, or popularity as substitutes for evidence.

## Unacceptable conduct

- harassment, threats, stalking, or discriminatory abuse;
- sexualized attention or imagery directed at participants;
- deliberate disclosure of private information;
- fabricated evidence, output, attribution, or endorsement;
- repeated disruption after a maintainer has set a clear procedural boundary;
- using the audit process to intimidate, diagnose, publicly rank, or target a person.

## Enforcement

Report sensitive conduct concerns through the repository’s private security or maintainer contact channel. The maintainer may remove content, restrict participation, or close an interaction to protect participants and the project. Decisions should be documented without exposing protected information. Good-faith technical disagreement is not misconduct.

This policy governs project spaces only. It does not adjudicate unrelated disputes or certify anyone’s character.
