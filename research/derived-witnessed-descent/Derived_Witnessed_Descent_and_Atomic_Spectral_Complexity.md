## Abstract

The present Boundary-State Field Calculus (BSC) catalogue has a defensible mathematical core: typed scientific contexts, certificate complexes, observation quotients, exact transport defects, quantitative discrepancy budgets, product-valued gates, and an atomic-rigidity boundary for arithmetic traces. Its next foundation is not another physical analogy. It is a homotopy-invariant theory of witnessed descent.

This note makes that step. The raw path-holonomy matrix currently used by the audit calculus is not the correct invariant for claims represented on homology. Two nonidentical certificate paths may differ by a chain homotopy and therefore induce exactly the same promoted claim. We replace raw holonomy by a derived class in the degree-zero homology of the mapping complex, give exact rational pass and fail certificates, introduce an observation-reduced version, and prove a factorization theorem over a presented context category. A quantitative harmonic residual measures the part of a path discrepancy that cannot be removed by chain homotopy.

Four additional bridges follow. First, observation loss admits an exact positive-semidefinite Fisher Gram certificate. Second, the discrepancy between finite truncation and full quantum evolution is exactly controlled by the off-diagonal boundary block of the generator. Third, a moving-boundary balance law separates local entropy change, volume growth, flux, and cumulative spacetime integration; this resolves the local/global ambiguity in the attached Gravity-from-Entropy audit. Fourth, an explicit shifted spectral ladder on \(\ell^2(\mathbb Z)\) passes trace-class resolvent convergence while its symmetric-cutoff relative traces converge to an infinite off-origin atomic comb. This proves that resolvent control is compatible with atomic trace formation but cannot certify arithmetic support, regularization independence, or provenance.

The note also repairs an ill-typed atomic-charge codomain, proves that distributionally closed smooth counterterms necessarily collapse to all distributions, derives a finite-spectral-ladder no-go for the prime-power comb, and isolates a conditional obstruction to naive orthogonal prime-block gluing. Every statement is classified. No Riemann-hypothesis proof, quantum-gravity theory, or completed prime-local operator is claimed.

## 3. Corrections required before extension

### 3.1 Raw path holonomy is a presentation-level invariant

The present note defines, for two paths \(p,q:c\to d\),

\[
\Omega_{p,q}=T_p-T_q
\]

and treats a nonzero column as a path-dependence witness. This is correct only for **strict chain-level equality**. It is too strong for claims represented by homology.

A nonzero \(\Omega\) may be null-homotopic. Then it changes certificate representatives while changing no homology class. Such a discrepancy must be preserved, but it must not automatically demote a homology-level claim.

Every future holonomy obligation therefore needs a declared mode:

- `strict`: require \(T_p=T_q\) as graded maps;
- `derived`: require equal induced maps on homology;
- `observed_derived`: require equality only after a lawful observation quotient.

### 3.2 Atomic charge needs locally finite coefficients

The atomic section places

\[
\operatorname{At}(T)=\sum_{t\in E}c_t[t]
\]

in the ordinary free complex vector space on \(\mathbb R\setminus\{0\}\). That vector space contains only finite formal sums. A prime-power comb has infinite, though locally finite, support. The displayed object is therefore ill-typed as written.

The correct codomain is

\[
C_0^{\mathrm{lf}}(\mathbb R\setminus\{0\};\mathbb C)
=
\left\{
c:\mathbb R\setminus\{0\}\to\mathbb C:
\operatorname{supp}(c)\cap K\text{ is finite for every compact }K
\right\}.
\]

Equivalently, one may use locally finite atomic complex Radon measures away from the origin. With this repair, the prime-power atomic charge is well-defined and prime independence remains valid coordinatewise by unique factorization.

### 3.3 Homology is not semantic for free

For any certificate complex \(K\) and any graded vector space \(V\),

\[
K'=K\oplus(V,0)
\]

retains all original matrices and certificates but changes

\[
H(K')\cong H(K)\oplus V.
\]

Thus the sentence "homology records unresolved obligations" is not intrinsic unless the construction of the complex is itself governed. A lawful certificate complex needs:

1. a content-addressed semantic source for every basis vector;
2. a canonical differential derived from typed incidence or dependency data;
3. a declared class of legal presentation refinements;
4. invariance under those refinements;
5. rejection of ungrounded zero-differential summands.

The natural target is therefore a homotopy category or a localization at declared semantic equivalences, not the category of raw matrix presentations.

### 3.4 Cumulative spacetime growth is not a spatial second law

For nested regions \(M_t\) and nonnegative density \(s\),

\[
S_4(t)=\int_{M_t}s\,dV
\]

is nondecreasing whenever the added shell has nonnegative integral. This can hold without a microscopic production law. It cannot alone imply increasing entropy of a spatial state, complexity, structure formation, or life.

The attached Gravity-from-Entropy audit correctly detects this boundary. Section 7 below supplies the exact balance law and its FRW specialization.

### 3.5 Endpoint support is not retained option value under noise

If a stochastic transition kernel has full support on the viable endpoint region, then every viable neighborhood has positive probability after one step. A reachable-future set defined only by endpoint support becomes the entire viable region for nearly every action. Its volume cannot discriminate policies.

The primitive must instead be a set of endpoint **laws** satisfying a chance-constrained or robust viability requirement, compared using a declared Wasserstein, deficiency, or decision metric.

## 4. Derived witnessed descent

### 4.1 Mapping complex

Let \(C=(C_n,d_n^C)\) and \(D=(D_n,d_n^D)\) be bounded finite-dimensional chain complexes over a computable field \(F\), normally \(\mathbb Q\). Define

\[
\underline{\operatorname{Hom}}_r(C,D)
=\bigoplus_n\operatorname{Hom}(C_n,D_{n+r})
\]

with differential

\[
\delta h=d_Dh-(-1)^rhd_C,
\qquad
\delta:\underline{\operatorname{Hom}}_r\to
\underline{\operatorname{Hom}}_{r-1}.
\]

Then \(\delta^2=0\). Degree-zero cycles are chain maps, and degree-zero boundaries are null-homotopic maps. We write

\[
\operatorname{Hol}(f,g)
=[f-g]
\in H_0\!\left(\underline{\operatorname{Hom}}(C,D)\right).
\]

Under cohomological reindexing this is the usual \(H^0\) mapping-complex class.

### Theorem 4.1 - Derived holonomy criterion

Let \(f,g:C\to D\) be chain maps. The following are equivalent:

1. \(H_n(f)=H_n(g)\) for every \(n\);
2. \(\operatorname{Hol}(f,g)=0\);
3. there exists a degree \(+1\) family \(h_n:C_n\to D_{n+1}\) such that

\[
f_n-g_n=d^D_{n+1}h_n+h_{n-1}d^C_n
\quad\text{for every }n.
\]

#### Proof

Condition 3 is the chain-homotopy identity, so it implies 1. It is equivalent to 2 by the definition of mapping-complex homology.

For \(1\Rightarrow3\), use that every finite-dimensional complex over a field splits as

\[
C_n=B_n(C)\oplus H_n^C\oplus L_n^C,
\]

where \(B_n(C)=\operatorname{im}d_{n+1}^C\), \(H_n^C\) represents homology, and

\[
d_n^C:L_n^C\xrightarrow{\sim}B_{n-1}(C).
\]

The same holds for \(D\). A chain map inducing zero on homology has zero harmonic block. Its remaining blocks lie in contractible summands, where the displayed isomorphisms construct a contracting homotopy. Apply this to \(f-g\). \(\square\)

The field hypothesis is essential. Acyclic complexes over general rings need not be contractible.

### Exact certificate format

Vectorize all entries of \(h_n\) into \(\mathbf h\) and all entries of \(\Omega=f-g\) into \(\boldsymbol\omega\). The homotopy equations become one rational system

\[
A\mathbf h=\boldsymbol\omega.
\]

Its blocks are built from

\[
I\otimes d^D_{n+1}
\quad\text{and}\quad
(d_n^C)^T\otimes I.
\]

- A pass certificate is rational \(\mathbf h\) with exact equality \(A\mathbf h=\boldsymbol\omega\).
- A fail certificate is rational \(y\) with

\[
y^TA=0,
\qquad
y^T\boldsymbol\omega\ne0.
\]

The second witness is a finite dual obstruction and is independently checkable by exact arithmetic.

### Minimal false-block witness

Take the contractible two-term complex

\[
0\longrightarrow\mathbb Q
\xrightarrow{\operatorname{id}}
\mathbb Q\longrightarrow0.
\]

Let \(f=\operatorname{id}\) and \(g=0\). The raw matrix \(\Omega=f-g\) is nonzero in both degrees, but every homology group is zero and \(\operatorname{id}=dh+hd\). A strict holonomy gate fails; a derived gate passes. This should be the first regression fixture for any future homotopy-aware checker.

### 4.2 Observation-reduced holonomy

Let \(N\subseteq D\) be an observation-null subcomplex:

\[
d_DN\subseteq N.
\]

The condition is necessary; without it \(D/N\) is not a chain complex. Write

\[
\pi:D\to\bar D=D/N.
\]

### Theorem 4.2 - Observed homotopy-holonomy criterion

For chain maps \(f,g:C\to D\), the following are equivalent:

\[
H_\bullet(\pi f)=H_\bullet(\pi g),
\]

\[
[\pi(f-g)]=0
\in H_0\!\left(\underline{\operatorname{Hom}}(C,\bar D)\right),
\]

and the existence of \(h:C_n\to\bar D_{n+1}\) satisfying

\[
\pi(f-g)=d_{\bar D}h+hd_C.
\]

#### Proof

Apply Theorem 4.1 to the two quotient chain maps \(\pi f\) and \(\pi g\). \(\square\)

This yields four distinct path states:

1. strict equality;
2. strict inequality but chain-homotopic equality;
3. homological inequality before observation but equality after observation;
4. inequality visible after observation.

Only state 4 is automatically fatal for an observation-level promoted claim. A stricter scientific claim may legitimately preregister state 1 or 2 as its requirement.

### 4.3 Query-holonomy Galois connection

Let \(V\subseteq H_n(D)\) be a holonomy subspace, for example

\[
V=\operatorname{im}\bigl(H_n(f)-H_n(g)\bigr).
\]

For linear query family \(Q\subseteq H_n(D)^*\), define

\[
\operatorname{Invis}(Q)=\bigcap_{q\in Q}\ker q,
\]

and

\[
\operatorname{Blind}(V)=\{q\in H_n(D)^*:q|_V=0\}.
\]

### Theorem 4.3 - Query-holonomy polarity

\[
Q\subseteq\operatorname{Blind}(V)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
V\subseteq\operatorname{Invis}(Q).
\]

#### Proof

Both statements say that every query in \(Q\) annihilates every vector in \(V\). \(\square\)

This is the linear, homology-level bridge between the existing observation Galois lattice and certificate path holonomy. It also identifies the smallest missing observation obligation: to detect all path effects on \(V\), an observation map \(O\) must satisfy

\[
V\cap\ker O=\{0\}.
\]

### 4.4 Factorization over context relations

Let \(G\) be a directed graph of contexts and elementary transports, and let \(\mathcal C\) be the category presented by \(G\) together with generating path relations \(p_i\sim q_i\). Assign a chain complex to each vertex and a chain map to each edge.

### Theorem 4.4 - Derived descent factorization

If, for every generating relation,

\[
T_{p_i}\simeq T_{q_i}
\]

by an exact chain homotopy, then the induced homology maps factor uniquely through a functor

\[
\bar H:\mathcal C\to\operatorname{GrVect}_F.
\]

If observation-null subcomplexes are supplied at every context and every elementary transport satisfies

\[
T_\alpha(N_c)\subseteq N_d,
\]

then the transports induce quotient chain maps. The same conclusion holds for observed homology whenever

\[
\pi T_{p_i}\simeq\pi T_{q_i}
\]

for every generator.

#### Proof

Chain-homotopic maps induce equal homology maps. Chain homotopy is preserved under precomposition and postcomposition by chain maps, so equality on the generating relations extends to the congruence they generate. The universal property of the presented category gives the factorization. \(\square\)

This theorem solves the earlier "which two-cells must be checked?" problem at the correct level: check a declared presentation, not every path pair. It does not automatically establish higher coherent homotopies; three-cell and higher obligations remain separate when the target semantics require them.

### 4.5 Quantitative homotopy residual

Equip every finite chain space with its declared standard Euclidean inner product in the registered basis, and use the resulting Frobenius norm. Define

\[
\eta(\Omega)=\min_h\|\Omega-(d_Dh+hd_C)\|_F.
\]

With the vectorized system above,

\[
\eta(\Omega)
=\min_{\mathbf h}\|\boldsymbol\omega-A\mathbf h\|_2
=\|P_{\ker A^*}\boldsymbol\omega\|_2.
\]

### Proposition 4.5 - Harmonic holonomy formula

For chain-map \(\Omega\), under the standard orthogonal Hodge splitting,

\[
\eta(\Omega)^2
=
\sum_n
\left\|
P_{\mathcal H_n(D)}\Omega_nP_{\mathcal H_n(C)}
\right\|_F^2.
\]

Thus \(\eta\) is exactly the size of the induced homology map represented on harmonic subspaces.

#### Certificate

For rational matrices in these Euclidean bases, solve the normal equations and record rational \(\mathbf h\) and residual

\[
\mathbf r=\boldsymbol\omega-A\mathbf h,
\qquad
A^T\mathbf r=0.
\]

Then

\[
\eta^2=\mathbf r^T\mathbf r\in\mathbb Q
\]

is an exact certificate. No floating pseudoinverse is required.

## 5. Quantitative observation bridge

Let \(X,Y\) be finite sets, let \(p_\theta(x)>0\) be a differentiable parametric state model, and let \(K(y\mid x)\) be a parameter-independent observation kernel. Define

\[
q_\theta(y)=\sum_xp_\theta(x)K(y\mid x).
\]

Restrict the output space to those \(y\) with \(q_\theta(y)>0\), or assume every output value used below has positive probability.

Let

\[
s_\theta(x)=\nabla_\theta\log p_\theta(x),
\qquad
t_\theta(y)=\nabla_\theta\log q_\theta(y).
\]

### Theorem 5.1 - Observation-loss Gram identity

Under the joint law \(p_\theta(x)K(y\mid x)\),

\[
t_\theta(Y)=\mathbb E_\theta[s_\theta(X)\mid Y]
\]

and

\[
J_X-J_Y
=
\mathbb E_\theta\!\left[
(s_\theta(X)-t_\theta(Y))
(s_\theta(X)-t_\theta(Y))^T
\right]
\succeq0.
\]

For a parameter direction \(v\),

\[
v^T(J_X-J_Y)v=0
\]

if and only if \(v^Ts_\theta(X)\) is measurable with respect to the observed variable \(Y\).

#### Proof

Differentiate \(q_\theta(y)\), divide by \(q_\theta(y)\), and obtain the conditional-expectation identity. Apply the conditional-variance decomposition to the score vector. \(\square\)

This converts observation-induced quotient loss into a positive-semidefinite certificate. At rational parameter points with rational probabilities, kernel entries, and derivatives, every entry is exactly rational.

The gate is local. Fisher information can vanish at first order while the model changes at second order. Therefore exact query descent, local information, and global identifiability remain separate coordinates.

## 6. Projected-unitary quantum boundary bridge

Let \(A\) be bounded self-adjoint on Hilbert space \(\mathcal H\), let \(P\) be an orthogonal finite-rank projection, and set \(Q=I-P\). Define

\[
A_P=PAP|_{P\mathcal H}
\]

and compare the two paths

\[
\text{evolve fully then observe}
\quad\text{and}\quad
\text{truncate then evolve}.
\]

Their curvature is

\[
\mathcal K_P(t)=Pe^{-itA}P-e^{-itA_P}.
\]

### Theorem 6.1 - Exact boundary-coupling formula

For every \(t\in\mathbb R\), with an oriented integral when \(t<0\),

\[
\mathcal K_P(t)
=
-i\int_0^t
e^{-i(t-s)A_P}PAQe^{-isA}P\,ds.
\]

Consequently,

\[
\|\mathcal K_P(t)\|
\le |t|\,\|PAQ\|,
\]

and, under the stated finite-rank hypothesis on \(P\), automatically

\[
\|\mathcal K_P(t)\|_1
\le |t|\,\|PAQ\|_1.
\]

Moreover,

\[
\mathcal K_P''(0)
=-PAQAP
=-(QAP)^*(QAP).
\]

Hence \(\mathcal K_P(t)=0\) on a neighborhood of zero if and only if \(QAP=0\), equivalently \(P\mathcal H\) is reducing for \(A\).

#### Proof

Differentiate \(Pe^{-itA}P\), insert \(I=P+Q\), and apply variation of constants relative to the finite generator \(A_P\). The norm estimates follow from unitary invariance. The second derivative follows from the Taylor expansions of the two exponentials. \(\square\)

The block \(QAP\) is a literal boundary ledger: it is exactly the dynamical coupling discarded by the finite closed model. Small coupling gives only finite-horizon control. In the two-level example

\[
A=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&\varepsilon\\
\varepsilon&0
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
P=
\begin{pmatrix}
1&0\\
0&0
\end{pmatrix},
\]

the discrepancy reaches \(2\) at \(t=\pi/\varepsilon\). Any quantum truncation certificate therefore requires an explicit time horizon.

## 7. Moving-boundary local/global promotion theorem

Let \(\Omega_t\subset\mathbb R^d\) be a smoothly moving domain with outward normal velocity \(v_n\), and let \(s(t,x)\) be an integrable scalar density. Define slice content

\[
S_\Sigma(t)=\int_{\Omega_t}s(t,x)\,dx.
\]

### Theorem 7.1 - Boundary-compensated promotion law

Whenever Reynolds transport applies,

\[
\frac{d}{dt}S_\Sigma(t)
=
\int_{\Omega_t}\partial_ts(t,x)\,dx
+
\int_{\partial\Omega_t}s(t,x)v_n(t,x)\,d\sigma.
\]

If \(\partial_ts\le0\), global slice growth occurs exactly when boundary/volume acquisition dominates local decay:

\[
\int_{\partial\Omega_t}sv_n\,d\sigma
\ge
-\int_{\Omega_t}\partial_ts\,dx.
\]

The two terms are separately meaningful certificates. Reporting only their sum erases whether growth came from intrinsic production or domain expansion.

### FRW specialization

For a homogeneous comoving region with physical volume

\[
V(t)=V_0a(t)^3,
\]

the slice content is \(S_\Sigma(t)=V(t)s(t)\), so

\[
\dot S_\Sigma(t)
=V_0a^3(\dot s+3Hs).
\]

If

\[
a(t)\propto t^{2/n},
\qquad
s(t)\propto t^{-2},
\]

then

\[
S_\Sigma(t)\propto t^{6/n-2}.
\]

The cumulative four-volume functional instead satisfies

\[
S_4(t)=C\int_{t_0}^t\tau^{6/n-2}\,d\tau
=
\begin{cases}
\displaystyle
\frac{C}{6/n-1}
\left(t^{6/n-1}-t_0^{6/n-1}\right), & n\ne6,\\[1.2ex]
C\log(t/t_0), & n=6.
\end{cases}
\]

It grows as a power for \(n<6\), grows logarithmically for \(n=6\), and approaches a finite limit with a correction of order \(t^{6/n-1}\) for \(n>6\).

- Matter domination: \(n=3\). The slice quantity is asymptotically constant while the cumulative quantity grows linearly.
- Radiation domination: \(n=4\). The slice quantity decreases like \(t^{-1/2}\) while the cumulative quantity grows like \(t^{1/2}\).

This exactly explains how a decreasing local density and increasing cumulative integral coexist. It also proves that cumulative monotonicity does not establish a conventional second law for spatial states. Such a claim needs a current \(J^a\), a production term \(\sigma\), a balance equation \(\nabla_aJ^a=\sigma\), boundary flux control, and an operational interpretation of the state entropy.

## 8. Counterterm topology and atomic provenance

### 8.1 Correct atomic object

Consider distributions that decompose away from the origin as

\[
T=C_0+a(u)\,du+\sum_{t\in E}c_t\delta_t,
\]

where \(\operatorname{supp}C_0\subseteq\{0\}\), \(a\in L^1_{\mathrm{loc}}\), and \(E\subset\mathbb R\setminus\{0\}\) is locally finite. Define

\[
\operatorname{At}(T)
=
\sum_{t\in E}c_t[t]
\in C_0^{\mathrm{lf}}(\mathbb R\setminus\{0\};\mathbb C).
\]

For the symmetric prime-power comb \(\mathcal P_p\), this charge is locally finite, and charges for distinct rational primes are linearly independent because

\[
m\log p=n\log q
\Longleftrightarrow
p^m=q^n
\Longrightarrow
p=q,\ m=n.
\]

### Theorem 8.1 - Topological counterterm collapse

Let \(\mathfrak C\subseteq\mathcal D'(\mathbb R)\) contain every compactly supported smooth function as a regular distribution. If \(\mathfrak C\) is sequentially closed for weak distributional convergence \(\sigma(\mathcal D',\mathcal D)\), then

\[
\mathfrak C=\mathcal D'(\mathbb R).
\]

#### Proof

For arbitrary \(T\in\mathcal D'(\mathbb R)\), let \(\rho_{\varepsilon_j}\) be a compactly supported approximate identity and let \(\chi_j\in C_c^\infty\) equal one on an expanding compact exhaustion. A diagonal sequence

\[
T_j=\chi_j(T*\rho_{\varepsilon_j})\in C_c^\infty(\mathbb R)
\]

converges to \(T\) against every test function in \(\mathcal D\). Since \(C_c^\infty\subseteq\mathfrak C\) and \(\mathfrak C\) is sequentially closed for this convergence, \(T\in\mathfrak C\). \(\square\)

Therefore no counterterm rule can simultaneously:

1. admit every smooth compactly supported counterterm;
2. be closed under unrestricted distributional limits;
3. exclude the prime-power comb.

A stronger compactness structure is mandatory.

### 8.2 Atom exclusion versus absolute-continuity preservation

The interval concentration modulus

\[
\sup_t\int_{t-\varepsilon}^{t+\varepsilon}|a_\lambda(u)|\,du
\to0
\]

excludes atoms, but it need not exclude a singular-continuous limit. If the admissible limiting counterterm must remain absolutely continuous, require uniform integrability on every compact \(K\):

\[
\upsilon_K(\delta)
=
\sup_\lambda
\sup_{\substack{E\subseteq K\\|E|\le\delta}}
\int_E|a_\lambda(u)|\,du
\longrightarrow0.
\]

A uniform local \(L^p\) bound with \(p>1\) gives the explicit certificate

\[
\int_E|a_\lambda|
\le C_K|E|^{1-1/p}.
\]

Thus BSC should distinguish two gates:

- `atomic_rigidity`: no off-origin atoms appear;
- `ac_counterterm_closure`: the limit remains absolutely continuous away from the origin.

## 9. Infinite-dimensional resolvent/trace separation

Let \(\mathcal H=\ell^2(\mathbb Z)\) with basis \(e_k\). Fix \(\alpha\in\mathbb R\setminus\mathbb Z\), and define self-adjoint operators on their common natural domain by

\[
Ae_k=ke_k,
\qquad
A^{(0)}e_k=(k+\alpha)e_k.
\]

Let \(P_N\) project onto \(\operatorname{span}\{e_k:|k|\le N\}\).

### Theorem 9.1 - Shifted-ladder resolvent/trace separation

For every \(z\in\mathbb C\setminus\mathbb R\),

\[
(A-z)^{-1}-(A^{(0)}-z)^{-1}\in\mathcal S_1,
\]

because its diagonal coefficients are

\[
\frac{1}{k-z}-\frac{1}{k+\alpha-z}
=
\frac{\alpha}{(k-z)(k+\alpha-z)}
=O(k^{-2}).
\]

The cutoff resolvent differences converge in trace norm, uniformly for \(z\) in compact subsets of \(\mathbb C\setminus\mathbb R\).

Nevertheless, the finite symmetric-cutoff relative unitary traces

\[
T_N(t)
=
\operatorname{Tr}\!\left[
P_N(e^{itA}-e^{itA^{(0)}})P_N
\right]
\]

satisfy

\[
T_N(t)
=(1-e^{i\alpha t})\sum_{k=-N}^Ne^{ikt}
\]

and converge in \(\mathcal S'(\mathbb R)\) to the cutoff-defined distribution

\[
2\pi\sum_{m\in\mathbb Z}
\left(1-e^{2\pi i\alpha m}\right)
\delta(t-2\pi m).
\]

The Fourier convention is

\[
\widehat\varphi(\xi)
=
\int_{\mathbb R}\varphi(t)e^{-it\xi}\,dt.
\]

The atom at the origin cancels, while infinitely many off-origin atoms remain.

#### Proof

Absolute summability of the diagonal resolvent difference gives the trace-class statement and compact-uniform tail bound. The symmetric Dirichlet kernels converge in tempered distributions by Poisson summation,

\[
\sum_{k\in\mathbb Z}e^{ikt}
=2\pi\sum_{m\in\mathbb Z}\delta(t-2\pi m).
\]

Multiplication by the smooth factor \(1-e^{i\alpha t}\) gives the stated limit. \(\square\)

This theorem has a precise interpretation:

- trace-norm resolvent convergence is compatible with atomic distributional traces;
- it is therefore compatible with a viable infinite-dimensional mechanism;
- the limiting unitary difference itself is not asserted to be trace class;
- it does not determine atomic locations, weights, arithmetic provenance, cutoff independence, or determinant existence;
- the exact raw prime increment and counterterm-rigidity gates remain logically independent.

### Definition 9.2 - Atomic spectral complexity

For a distribution \(\mu\) whose off-origin atomic charge is locally finite, define its positive atomic support complexity by

\[
\mathcal C_\mu(T)
=
\#\left(
\operatorname{supp}\operatorname{At}(\mu)\cap(0,T]
\right).
\]

This is a support invariant, not an entropy or computational-complexity claim. It measures how rapidly distinct positive atomic locations accumulate.

### Corollary 9.3 - Constant-multiplicity finite spectral-ladder no-go

Consider a finite union of bilateral arithmetic spectral ladders with constant finite multiplicity and weight on each progression. Assume symmetric cutoffs and relative shifts, so each cutoff trace limit is a smooth phase multiplier times the corresponding Dirac comb. Its atomic time support is contained in a finite union of reciprocal lattices

\[
\bigcup_{j=1}^J\frac{2\pi}{h_j}\mathbb Z.
\]

Hence \(\mathcal C_\mu(T)=O(T)\) for this ladder class. The positive prime-power support contains at least \(\log p\) for every \(p\le e^T\), so its complexity satisfies

\[
\#\{m\log p\le T:p\text{ prime},\ m\ge1\}
\ge\pi(e^T)
\sim\frac{e^T}{T}.
\]

Therefore no finite union in this constant-multiplicity arithmetic-ladder class can produce the full prime-power delta comb. Arbitrary index-dependent weights are outside this corollary and require separate analysis.

The operator architecture must contain genuinely non-lattice spectral organization or an infinite family of controlled components.

### 9.4 Conditional orthogonal-prime obstruction

Suppose a proposed fold decomposes into mutually orthogonal prime blocks,

\[
B_S(z)=\bigoplus_{p\in S}B_p(z).
\]

Then

\[
\|B_T(z)-B_S(z)\|_1
=
\sum_{p\in T\setminus S}\|B_p(z)\|_1.
\]

Consequently, trace-norm Cauchy convergence is equivalent to absolute summability of the local block norms.

If the exact prime trace law and an admissible noncancellation estimate imply

\[
|\operatorname{Tr}B_p(i\eta)|
\ge
c\frac{\log p}{p^{1/2+\eta}-1},
\]

then

\[
\sum_p\|B_p(i\eta)\|_1=\infty
\qquad(0<\eta\le1/2).
\]

This would rule out the naive orthogonal direct-sum prime fold. The implication is a valid theorem schema, but its premise has not been proved for the desired relative trace construction. Origin-supported and absolutely continuous counterterm cancellation, the unitary-trace/resolvent transform, and uniformity in \(p\) remain open obligations.

## 10. The derived witnessed descent object

Fix a computable exact field \(F\). For each context \(c\), require finite-dimensional exact presentations of \(K_\bullet(c)\), \(N_\bullet(c)\), their differentials and inclusions, and every generating transport. Define the fiber

\[
\mathfrak A(c)
=
\bigl(
K_\bullet(c),
N_\bullet(c),
\mathcal G_c,
I_c,
\mathcal B_c
\bigr),
\]

where:

- \(K_\bullet(c)\) is a semantically generated certificate complex;
- \(N_\bullet(c)\) is an observation-null subcomplex;
- \(\mathcal G_c\) is the product-valued gate record;
- \(I_c:H_\bullet(K(c)/N(c))\to V_c\) is a homology-level invariant into a finite rational presentation \(V_c\);
- \(\mathcal B_c\) is a quantitative defect budget.

For a context arrow \(\alpha:c\to d\), supply:

- a graded certificate transport \(T_\alpha\);
- compatibility \(T_\alpha(N(c))\subseteq N(d)\), producing an observed quotient transport \(\bar T_\alpha\);
- a rational invariant transport \(\iota_\alpha:V_c\to V_d\);
- an affine discrepancy certificate.

Before edge legality is established, the raw witnessed obstruction record is

\[
\mathfrak W_{\mathrm{raw}}(\alpha;p,q)
=
\left(
\Theta_\alpha,
\Omega_{p,q},
\mathcal B_\alpha
\right),
\]

with

\[
\Theta_\alpha=dT_\alpha-T_\alpha d,
\qquad
J_\alpha
=
I_d\,H(\bar T_\alpha)-\iota_\alpha I_c.
\]

The expression for \(J_\alpha\) is evaluated only after \(\Theta_\alpha=0\) and observation-null compatibility have produced \(H(\bar T_\alpha)\). Likewise, the derived record

\[
\mathfrak W_{\mathrm{der}}(p,q)
=
\left(
[\Omega_{p,q}],
[\pi\Omega_{p,q}],
J_\alpha,
\eta(\pi\Omega_{p,q})
\right)
\]

is defined only when both path transports are chain maps and the quotient transports exist. Bracketed classes are never formed from untyped raw discrepancies.

### Theorem 10.1 - Finite witnessed descent

Suppose:

1. \(\Theta_\alpha=0\) and \(T_\alpha(N(c))\subseteq N(d)\) for every generating context arrow \(\alpha:c\to d\);
2. observed derived holonomy vanishes for every generating path relation;
3. each \(I_c\) is defined on observed homology, hence is invariant under chain homotopy;
4. \(J_\alpha=0\) for every generating arrow;
5. the maps \(\iota_\alpha\) compose on paths and satisfy \(\iota_{p_i}=\iota_{q_i}\) for every generating path relation;
6. the complexes \(K(c)\), null subcomplexes \(N(c)\), generating transports, spaces \(V_c\), maps \(I_c\), and maps \(\iota_\alpha\) all have finite exact presentations over \(F\) (rational presentations when \(F=\mathbb Q\));
7. every applicable fatal gate is computed as `pass` from complete, nonconflicting evidence;
8. every quantitative path budget remains below its preregistered threshold.

Then verified observed homology classes factor functorially through the presented finite context category, the \(V_c\) and \(\iota_\alpha\) define an invariant functor on that category, and the \(I_c\) form a natural transformation between them. Every failure of conditions 1, 2, 4, or 5 has a finite exact witness: a nonzero chain-defect column; a basis vector \(n\in N(c)\) with \(\pi_dT_\alpha n\ne0\); a left-null homotopy obstruction; or a nonzero invariant-coordinate mismatch.

#### Proof

Condition 1 gives observed quotient chain maps. Theorem 4.4 and condition 2 give factorization of observed homology. Condition 5 makes the invariant transports factor through the same path relations. Conditions 3 and 4 then give the naturality squares. Finite rational presentation supplies coordinate witnesses. Gate and budget conclusions follow from the declared conjunctive logic and associative affine composition. \(\square\)

This theorem is finite. Locally finite atomic charge requires a compact exhaustion or registered separating coordinate family rather than a single finite \(V_c\). Passage to a directed limit requires separate compactness, topology, trace, and cofinal-path obligations. Finite functoriality must not be promoted to limit existence.

## 15. Final mathematical verdict

The BSC programme now has a clearer foundation than it did at the start of this review.

The decisive advance is the recognition that certificate path dependence has three levels: strict, derived, and observed-derived. The current raw holonomy is useful but not invariant under harmless contractible refinements. Passing to mapping-complex homology removes presentation noise while retaining exact rational witnesses. This is the correct mathematical bridge between certificate transport, observation descent, and context coherence.

The operator analysis contributes a second boundary. Infinite-dimensional trace-class resolvent control can genuinely generate atomic distributional traces, but it cannot determine their arithmetic content. The prime program therefore needs three independent certificates:

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal S_1\text{ resolvent convergence}
+
\text{raw atomic support and weights}
+
\text{counterterm nonconcentration/closure}
}
\]

The moving-boundary theorem supplies the corresponding physical lesson: local change, boundary acquisition, slice content, and cumulative spacetime content are different typed objects. A rigorous theory must transport each separately.

What has become clear is not a universal theory of nature. It is a stronger theorem architecture: **derived witnessed descent**, coupled to **atomic provenance** and **boundary-compensated promotion**. That architecture is concrete, falsifiable, certificate-producing, and broad enough to connect the catalogue without forcing unrelated physical systems into one ontology.
